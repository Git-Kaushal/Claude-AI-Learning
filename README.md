# Working with Claude: a 4-week course (Sample edition)

Original instructional design & content by **Kaushal J** · Education Strategist · AI in Education · © 2026 Kaushal J. Sample edition.

A practical, week-by-week course for **higher-education faculty and working professionals in any field**. Every lesson pairs a real use case with numbered steps to do in Claude, ready-to-fill prompt templates, practice and a short quiz. Feature descriptions follow the Claude Help Center (checked October 2026); Claude's menus and plans change, so each lesson links to the matching Help Center article.
This sample edition opens Week 1 (3 lessons) and 2 of 4 studio tools.

## How the course runs

1. **Week by week.** Weeks open in order: a week opens when every lesson in the previous week is complete.
2. **Step by step.** A lesson is complete when its quiz is passed with 60% or more.
3. **Final stage**, after Week 4: the course mind map, then the adaptive assessment.
4. **Certificate.** Participants who answer at least 10 assessment questions with 70% or more correct can download a certificate of completion (PDF).
5. **Results.** Participants can download only a results PDF (scores and mastery, no questions or answers) to send to the trainer. The trainer view loads these PDFs.

Progress is stored only in the participant's own browser on their device.
